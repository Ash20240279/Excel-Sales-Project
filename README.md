# Sales Data Analysis Dashboard 📊

## Overview
An interactive sales analysis dashboard built using Google Sheets to explore sales performance, product profitability, customer activity, and monthly sales trends.

The project transforms raw sales records into visual insights that help identify top-performing products, leading sales representatives, and regional sales patterns.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across different products and regions.
- Identify the best-selling products and top sales representatives.
- Explore customer purchasing patterns.
- Track monthly sales trends.
- Evaluate product profitability and support data-driven business decisions.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis
Visualize sales data to understand overall performance and compare results across different categories.

### 2. Top 5 Sales Representatives
Identify the leading sales representatives based on their sales performance.

### 3. Best-Selling Products
Compare product performance to highlight the products contributing most to sales.

### 4. Monthly Sales Trends
Track sales across the months of the year to identify fluctuations and potential seasonal patterns.

### 5. Net Profit Analysis
Compare net profit across products to identify more profitable items.

### 6. Regional Sales Analysis
Explore sales distribution across geographical regions and countries.

### 7. Interactive Filtering
Use dashboard filters to explore different subsets of the available data.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Google Sheets** — Data exploration, analysis, and dashboard creation.
- **Charts & Visualizations** — Presenting sales trends and business insights.
- **Spreadsheet Functions** — Supporting data analysis and reporting.

## Dataset
The dataset contains sales transaction records with fields covering order information, order dates, order status, geographical details, shipping information, product categories, product names, customer details, and numerical sales-related attributes.

## Business Value
This dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance and helps users:
- Recognize high-performing products and sales representatives.
- Monitor sales trends over time.
- Compare performance across regions.
- Investigate product profitability.
- Make more informed business decisions.

## Project Preview

The dashboard includes visualizations for sales representatives, best-selling products, monthly sales, net profit, and regional performance.

## How to Use
1. Open the Google Sheets dashboard.
2. Explore the available charts and visualizations.
3. Apply the interactive filters to investigate specific segments.
4. Use the resulting insights to evaluate sales performance.
